🏛️ About V1

BattleForge V1 is an experimental historical probability simulator. Force ratings are simplified modeling values rather than claims of exact historical measurement.

The engine compares force characteristics, terrain compatibility, condition and controlled randomness across repeated simulations.

Future versions can add historical battles, commanders, additional eras, campaigns and deeper historical datasets.
